Why external wall insulation

Insulate the house without giving up a room.

External wall insulation goes on the outside of your walls. The house is wrapped from the outside, so the inside of your home stays as it is.

Warmth stays in

Outside walls lose heat. A layer of insulation across the whole wall helps keep the heat you pay for inside the house.

No space lost indoors

Because the insulation is fixed to the outside, your rooms keep their size and your skirting, sockets and radiators stay where they are.

A new face for the house

Dated pebbledash or tired render is covered by a fresh acrylic render finish, so the house looks new as well as feeling warmer.

The work happens outside

The job is done from the outside of the house, so you can keep living in your home while it happens.
