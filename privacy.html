Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to the details you send us through the quote form on this website.

What we collect

When you send the quote form, we receive:

  • your name and phone number
  • your area or Eircode
  • your email address, type of house and message, if you choose to give them

How your details reach us

The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service. It passes your details to our email address, fiona.abac@gmail.com.

What we use them for

We only use your details to reply to your enquiry, to arrange and give you a quote, and, if you go ahead, to apply for the SEAI grant on your behalf and carry out the work.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, or ask us to delete them, by emailing fiona.abac@gmail.com or calling 086 365 4911.
